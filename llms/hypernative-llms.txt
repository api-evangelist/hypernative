# Hypernative

> Hypernative is the institutional-grade Web3 security platform that stops threats before they cause damage — protecting protocols, chains, asset managers, exchanges, wallets, and financial institutions with real-time monitoring, detection, and automated onchain response.

Hypernative secures $100B+ in onchain assets across 250+ customers and 70+ chains. It detects 99.5% of hacks, with 98% detected 2+ minutes before the first hack transaction, at a false positive rate below 0.001%. To date, Hypernative has saved over $2B in funds from exploits.

## Products

- [Hypernative Platform](https://www.hypernative.io/product/onchain-monitoring-automated-response): Real-time monitoring, detection, and automated onchain response. Detects the widest range of Web3 attacks with high accuracy.
- [Guardian](https://www.hypernative.io/product/transaction-guard): Real-time transaction simulation, analysis, and policy enforcement. Eliminates phishing and fraud risk before signing.
- [Screener](https://www.hypernative.io/product/screening-intelligence): Real-time address reputation and risk scoring across multiple chains.
- [Wallet Protect](https://www.hypernative.io/product/wallet-protection): Enterprise-grade threat prevention for individual wallet users.
- [Fraud Prevention](https://www.hypernative.io/product/fraud-prevention): Transaction-level policy enforcement to keep attackers out without disrupting legitimate users.
